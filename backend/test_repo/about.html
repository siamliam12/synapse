The Beginning

From a Notebook
to 75 Countries

In 2009, founder Elena Vasquez sat in a rain-soaked café in Lisbon, realising that the best experiences of her five-year journey through 40 countries had one thing in common: they were all accidents — moments no guidebook anticipated.

She opened a notebook and began writing down every local she'd befriended, every hidden restaurant, every unpublicised festival, every back road that led somewhere remarkable. That notebook became Wanderlux.

Fifteen years later, that spirit of serendipitous discovery — curated, perfected, and delivered with absolute reliability — remains at the heart of every journey we craft.

What We Believe

Our Four
Guiding Truths

01

Authenticity Over Aesthetics

We resist the Instagram-ification of travel. Our itineraries prioritise meaning, depth, and genuine encounter over photogenic but hollow experiences.

02

Slow Travel as Luxury

We deliberately resist the urge to pack more in. The finest journeys unfold at a pace that allows the unexpected to find you — and it always does.

03

Leave It Better

Every trip we arrange funds conservation projects, employs local guides at fair wages, and chooses accommodation that reinvests in the community.

04

Radically Personalised

We have never sent two clients on the same trip. Not once. The earth is vast enough — your journey should be entirely, uniquely yours.
